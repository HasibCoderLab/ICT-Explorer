01 / THE FOUNDATION

First, understand
what ICT really means.

ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology. It includes the digital tools and systems we use to create, store, process and exchange information.

01

Information

Meaningful data that helps people learn, understand and make decisions.

02

Communication

Sharing information between people and devices across distances.

03

Technology

Tools, devices and systems that help us solve problems and do work.

04 / DIGITAL RESPONSIBILITY

Be connected.
Stay protected.

Using technology wisely also means understanding online risks. Learn to recognize suspicious messages and protect personal information.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Think before sharing personal information.
  • Check links and senders before clicking.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.
